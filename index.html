Ritual

Five steps, two minutes.

Do it properly once and it becomes automatic. Keep scrolling to walk through it.

01

Pick a scent

Each tin holds four. Cedar for a long evening, peppermint for a slow morning, lemongrass when the kitchen needs clearing.

02

Light the tip

Touch it to a match or lighter and wait until a small flame stands up on its own.

03

Fan the flame out

Wave it gently with your hand until only an ember is left glowing. Don't blow on it, or you'll scatter ash across the table.

04

Set it in the holder

Angle the stick into the burner so the ash drops where you want it, and not onto the shelf below.

05

Let it finish

Once the stick has burned down to ash, tap the holder clean and start again tomorrow.
